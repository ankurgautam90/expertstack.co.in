    Built for what's next. Ready now. 

    While others promise tomorrow, Algorand is ready with trusted infrastructure for agentic commerce, institutional-grade DeFi, and a clear path to quantum resilience today. Are you ready?

    Our roadmap to broad quantum resilience by 2027

    Algorand has been the leader in blockchain quantum resilience since implementing Falcon signatures in 2022 to safeguard the entire history of its chain against the potential threat of quantum computers.

    In 2025, we extended this further by executing the first quantum‑resistant transaction on Mainnet using Falcon signatures. But we aren't stopping there. See our path to achieving broad quantum resilience by 2027.

    Commerce, run by agents

    The future of commerce won't be executed by humans alone. AI agents will discover services, negotiate terms, and complete transactions independently. Algorand's fast, reliable, and low-cost settlement infrastructure gives agents the ability to transact in real time and keep workflows moving without interruption.

    DeFi works better on Algorand

    DeFi needs infrastructure that just works. Algorand delivers instant settlement, atomic execution, and predictable outcomes, so you can ship real financial applications — not patch around limitations.

    The premier blockchain for RWA tokenization

    Many blockchains support tokens, but few can support the rigors of institutional finance.

    Ready to bring real-world assets on-chain? Algorand delivers the performance, security, and efficiency needed to tokenize bonds, equities, funds, real estate, and structured products at scale.

    A better way to deliver aid payments

    Leading on post-quantum technology

    Algorand has been leading in blockchain quantum resilience since 2022. By implementing NIST-selected Falcon signatures, Algorand already safeguards the entire history of its chain against the future threat of quantum computers.

    In 2025, we further extended PQ readiness by executing the first quantum‑resistant transactions on mainnet. This expanded the network's PQ protections to real digital assets on a live public blockchain.

    And now, we have announced our roadmap to target broad quantum resilience by the end of 2027 – including native PQ accounts with related SDK and developer tooling support; PQ multisig for institutions, treasuries, and high-value operations; and research into PQ-resilient VRF and signatures for consensus messaging. The first milestones from this roadmap begin in Q3 2026.

    Everything you need in Layer-1

    The Algorand blockchain has everything you need to build and scale dApps directly on Layer-1. It’s powered by Pure Proof-of-Stake for secure, efficient consensus and the Algorand Virtual Machine (AVM) for trustless, reliable smart contract execution. Ready for even the most demanding use cases.

    10,000+ transactions per second

    Pure Proof-of-Stake relies on randomness, eliminating the need for complex competition among network validators and enabling Algorand to handle high transaction volume without compromising on speed or security.

    Discover scalability that's ready to match your ambition.

    Instant finality

    No rollbacks + no partitioning = no uncertainty.

    One of Algorand's most powerful properties is instant finality, meaning transactions are confirmed immediately and never rolled back. In practice, over 10,000 transactions per second can be finalized the moment they enter a block. The result: the state of the ledger and every account is always clear.

    Ready for certainty? You're ready for instant finality.

    0 downtime in 7 years (and counting)

    When billions of dollars, critical applications, and real-world assets are on the line, reliability matters. Algorand has delivered uninterrupted network availability since launch, with zero downtime in more than seven years of operation.

    Always on. Ready when you are.

    True decentralization

    Algorand is designed to make staking simple and accessible — anyone with as little as 1 Algo can participate and help keep the network secure. Its Pure Proof-of-Stake consensus enables broad network participation, helping prevent concentration of power while strengthening resilience.

    The result is a blockchain that remains decentralized as it grows.

    Resilient by design. Ready for everyone.

    Secure by design. Leading on quantum.

    Security starts at the protocol level. Algorand's Pure Proof-of-Stake consensus uses cryptographic sortition to randomly select validators, making it extraordinarily difficult for any single actor to influence the network.

    Algorand has also led the industry in blockchain quantum resilience since 2022, and in June 2026, revealed a clear roadmap to broad quantum resilience by 2027.

    Secure today. Ready for tomorrow.

    Built-in, not bolted on.

    Many blockchains require developers to recreate common functionality in smart contracts, adding complexity, cost, and risk.

    Algorand provides powerful capabilities — like atomic transfers, whitelisting, role-based access control (RBAC), asset freeze and revoke, and more — directly at the protocol layer, helping developers build faster, write less code, and reduce potential vulnerabilities.

    Less code. More possibilities. Ready to build.

    See who's building on Algorand

    Businesses across the globe are leveraging the Algorand blockchain to deliver efficiency, impact, and opportunity at scale. Built for real-world use and proven in production, Algorand is ready for what's next.

    Ready to scale participation in the real estate market?

    Lofty is ready. This YC-backed startup is using Algorand to power a real estate tokenization platform that enables anyone to invest in shares of rental properties for as little as $50.

    Ready to reshape humanitarian aid?

    HesabPay is ready. With partners like WFP and MercyCorps, this payments startup enables more efficient, transparent aid delivery in Afghanistan and beyond through stablecoin-based payments on Algorand.

    Ready to bank without banks?

    Folks Finance is ready. From lending and borrowing to swaps, liquid staking, and more, Folks Finance users can experience the true benefits of DeFi with Algorand's high throughput, low fees, and proven reliability.

    Ready to unlock credit for millions of informal economy workers?

    Mann Deshi is ready. This cooperative bank is helping rural Indian women access formal credit through a Tokenized Credit Scorecard on Algorand — turning informal assets and transaction records into portable, on-chain proof of creditworthiness.

    Ready to give everyone a stake in the clean energy future?

    Enel is ready. Learn how the world's largest private electricity distribution company used Algorand to expand access to sustainable energy through fractional ownership of solar and wind infrastructure.
